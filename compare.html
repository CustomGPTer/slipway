What Slipway does not do

Slipway does not download apps or browse app sites; it installs files you already have. It is not on Google Play, so it does not update itself. Locked APKM files made by newer APKMirror apps cannot be opened.

Where the others do more

APKMirror Installer can send packages from a phone to an Android TV and has layouts for TVs and tablets. Both install and update from Google Play.
