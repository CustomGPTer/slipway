**First release.** A private installer for APK, XAPK (with OBB game data), APKS, APKM and ZIP files, with no internet access.

- Safety report before every install: red, amber or green verdict, version compare, Android and processor compatibility, developer signature, 51 known trackers found offline, every permission in plain English and the file's SHA-256 with a paste-to-check box.
- Library of the folders you choose (or all files), grouped by app with every revision and Update, Installed, Older, Not installed and Problem tags; search, filters, sort and multi-select.
- Install queue with reorder and retry, only the splits your phone needs, Android's errors explained with the fix, one-tap updates on Android 12 and later, and optional Shizuku mode with no prompts.
- Installed tab with search, filters, uninstall, batch uninstall and app backups as .apk or .apks; Clean up for duplicates and older revisions.
- History with PDF and CSV export, optional New APK alerts, home-screen widget and Newest APK tile, app lock, hide in recents, and Graphite, black, wallpaper and light themes.

Needs Android 10 or later. Size 3.2 MB (3,387,712 bytes).
