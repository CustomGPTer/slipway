Privacy policy

Last updated 26 November 2026. This policy covers the Slipway app for Android (package uk.jj.slipway) and this website. Slipway is made by Jonathan Jackson, an individual developer in the United Kingdom, who can be reached by opening an issue on the project's GitHub page.

The short version

  • Slipway does not hold Android's internet permission. It cannot go online, so it cannot send anything anywhere.
  • Your APK files, your list of apps, your install history and your settings stay on your phone.
  • There are no accounts, no servers, no advertising and no analytics.

What stays on your phone

Slipway keeps your settings, the folders you chose, what it has read from your APK files (so the library opens quickly), app icons and your install history in its private storage. Android's cloud backup and device transfer are switched off for Slipway. Backups and history exports you save go where you choose, and are yours to keep, move or delete.

Files you save

App backups and PDF or CSV history files are made only when you ask, and saved where you choose. Once saved, they are yours to keep, share or delete.

Links to this website

Settings, About has More apps, Check for a newer version, Website and Licences. Each opens this site in your browser; Slipway itself never goes online.

Keeping and deleting your data

History can be cleared in the History tab. Uninstalling Slipway deletes everything in its private storage. Your APK files are never deleted unless you ask, in Clean up or with Delete file after install.

Your rights

Because the developer holds no data about you, there is nothing to request, correct or erase on a server. Everything is already in your hands.

This website

This site is hosted by GitHub Pages. It uses no cookies and no analytics. The only script on it unlocks the download once the lawful-use box is ticked. GitHub may keep standard web server logs, such as IP addresses, under GitHub's privacy statement.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be raised on GitHub. This policy was last updated on 26 November 2026.
